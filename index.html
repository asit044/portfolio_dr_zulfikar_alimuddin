What I work on

Six areas where the work concentrates.

The roles change, the focus does not. Each area below is a practical answer to the same question — how do you build capability that outlives the person who built it?

01

Leadership Development

Growing leaders who can hold complexity — designing programmes, coaching executives, and building the internal pipeline an organisation needs before it needs it.

02

Organisational Transformation

Redesigning business processes and decision-making so efficiency and effectiveness improve together, rather than trading against each other.

03

Teacher & Education Development

Building learning communities and innovation ecosystems for teachers, so classroom practice keeps improving long after any single training ends.

04

Vocational & Higher Education

Shaping schools and polytechnics that stay relevant to the era their graduates actually enter — curriculum, governance, and industry linkage as one system.

05

Governance & Human Capital

Committee-level work on leadership evaluation, director performance, human capital policy, and the improvement priorities that follow from both.

06

Social Impact

Long-term support for children with special needs and underprivileged communities — treated as sustained commitment, not a campaign.
